# Sentrell Group — AI for Small Business: Win Back 5 Hours a Week

**Offer:** 90-minute, onsite or live instructor-led workshop for Kansas City business owners, operators and event-host communities.  
**Teaching tools:** Vendor-neutral; participants may use Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT or another employer-approved AI assistant.  
**No prerequisites:** No coding, automations or paid AI upgrade required to participate in core activities. Some platform features may vary by account or license.

## Program promise
Learn to identify a repetitive task, build a useful first draft, verify claims, and determine whether AI + review saves time. The title's five hours per week is an aspirational benchmark, not a promised customer result.

## Event description (approved copy)
**Win back time without handing over judgment.** In this interactive 90-minute session, participants will learn the basics of using approved AI assistants for customer emails, meeting follow-ups, and repetitive administration. Instead of copying magic prompts, they'll write their own instructions using Goal, Context, Source and Expectations. They'll practice spotting fabricated claims and take home a seven-day plan to measure time reclaimed. No technical background required; bring a laptop and access to an AI tool you are permitted to use.

## Who it serves
- Owners, office managers, coordinators and small-business operators with little or no practical AI experience.
- Chambers, coworking spaces, entrepreneurial programs and business networks looking for useful introductory training.
- Existing teams that need low-risk AI practices and a baseline for a future implementation sprint.

## Delivery format and accessibility
- Format: 1 instructor, ideally 10–20 participants; larger groups require another facilitator.
- Venue: reliable Wi-Fi, projector, screen, accessible room, microphone when needed.
- Students: bring laptop and an approved tool account; provide paper alternative if logins fail.
- Instructor: bring pre-tested fictional examples, offline PDF field guides, timer and backup browser/connection.
- No participant needs to provide customer records, confidential business information or government data to the public demonstration.
- Keep a low-motion, high-contrast display; verbally describe the interface; read out questions and allow silent written responses.
- No live AI account guarantee: ChatGPT and Copilot interfaces, plans and permissions vary. Instructor validates controls in the tenant used that day.

## Four-point practical assessment
A learner passes the workshop when they can independently:
1. Produce a prompt that contains the task, relevant context, source facts and output expectations.
2. Identify at least one invented/unsupported detail in an AI-generated draft.
3. Preserve a restriction without converting it into an unauthorized action.
4. Describe or demonstrate a method for timing manual vs reviewed AI-assisted work.

**Do not award completion solely for attendance.** In-person participants may use either ChatGPT or Copilot; score the observable skill, not app familiarity.

## Seven-day transfer protocol
- Select a low-risk recurring task; record 3 manual durations.
- Complete 3 comparable tasks with AI and human verification; record total durations and observed errors.
- Compare median duration per task, multiply by weekly frequency. Flag workloads and quality differences.
- Stop the workflow when errors, rework, cost or confidentiality risk outweigh savings.
- Attendee data stays with the attendee unless they separately consent to anonymous aggregate reporting.
